About Me

Hey! I am Manju Kashika, a Cognitive Neuroscience graduate who really likes research and art. After my BSc Psychology from India, switching to a more coding, stats and anatomy focused MSc Cognitive Neuroscience course in the UK was a pain in the tail. I also forget things. Not a great combination for someone aspiring to be a researcher.

The மீன் / Mean project is for me to document the process of figuring out advanced mathematics, statistics, physics for neuroscience research application, for my own goldfish memory and other fellow fish floating through space tryna explore the intricacies of sciences. I am hoping to put my art, research skills and blind perseverance to making this work for myself and hopefully for a few others. WISH ME LUCK. T_T

Goldfish Diary

Despite popular myth, goldfish can apparently hold onto memories for months— but I have realized my long-term memory is lacking the length (ironic that my research interest is memory). The goldfish diary is my public study log and self-curated curriculum. While ONE textbook or online course is not gonna do it all, it is all out there and this journey is gonna take a long time. Should be a fun new nerd adventure.
